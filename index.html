OUR STORY

We started Lumien for one curious kid.

Homeschooling gives kids room to follow their curiosity — but it asks a lot from parents. We built Lumien to carry the planning and teaching load, so families can spend more time on the wonder.

Lumien began as a side project between two friends who kept hearing the same thing from homeschool parents: the hardest part isn't loving the idea of homeschooling — it's the daily grind of planning, teaching, and tracking progress across every subject, every day.

We thought that grind was exactly the kind of problem an attentive, adaptive AI could help carry — not by replacing the parent, but by standing alongside them as a second teacher who never runs out of patience.
