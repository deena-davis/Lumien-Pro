Lumien watches the work, finds the gaps, and teaches the way your child actually learns — so you can stop guessing and start seeing progress.

Snap a photo of any worksheet. Lumien grades it instantly, marks the working, and explains every mistake in plain language.

02

Agentic Tutor

The moment a gap appears, Spark auto-generates a remedial quiz targeted at exactly the skill your child missed.

Eight proven teaching methods, auto-matched per child and per subject as their profile evolves.
