Who we are

Brighter minds, brighter future.

Lumien started with a question two homeschool families kept asking each other: is the work actually landing, or does it just look like it is? Nobody could answer it honestly — so we built something that could.

The origin story

We wanted to see what others couldn't.

A parent can mark a page. A parent cannot see the invisible chain of prerequisites behind a wrong answer. Tutors are expensive, weekly, and gone by Tuesday.

We wanted a co-teacher that sits beside the family every single day, notices the small cracks early, and quietly fixes them before they become a lost year.

From answers to understanding.

Lumien looks beyond the page to understand what a child actually knows.

Our mission

Give every homeschool family the power to see clearly.

Give every homeschool family the diagnostic power of a full school assessment team — in a pocket, for the price of a couple of coffees, with a firefly that makes the whole thing feel like play.
